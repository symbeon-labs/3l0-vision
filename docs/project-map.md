# 3L0 Vision — Project Map

## Mission

**Tornar o mundo físico computável.**

3L0 Vision converts observations from the physical world and existing enterprise information into traceable operational identity, state and action.

## Product equation

**OBSERVAR + RELACIONAR + RESOLVER + OPERAR**

## Strategic layers

A — Physical capture: camera, barcode, OCR, documents, optional QR/NFC and human input.

B — Observation: normalized representation of what a source observed or extracted.

C — Resolution: ORC combines assertions, evidence, entities, relations and context.

D — Operational identity: persistent entities, identifiers, attributes, provenance and history.

E — Workflow: receiving, inventory, transfer, return, fulfillment and related logistics operations.

F — Enterprise execution: ERP, fiscal systems, supplier systems and downstream actions.

G — Evaluation: telemetry, quality, failure analysis, economics and auditability.

## Product loop

WORLD → CAPTURE → OBSERVATION → MATCH → RESOLUTION → IDENTITY → OPERATIONAL STATE → ACTION → EVIDENCE/HISTORY

History feeds future resolution.

## Core principle

**The operator works on exceptions, not on reconstructing information the system already has.**

## Strategic differentiation hypothesis

The product is not primarily differentiated by camera, OCR, barcode scanning or ERP integration individually.

The hypothesis is that value emerges from combining them around a persistent resolution layer that preserves source information, relates representations, exposes uncertainty, resolves within context, asks humans only when necessary, produces reusable identity and preserves the path from evidence to action.

This must be validated in the field.

## Relationship with ORC

**ORC = research + semantics + resolution.**

**3L0 Vision = product + workflow + operator.**

3L0 consumes validated ORC semantics rather than redefining them.

## Build strategy

Progress vertically before expanding horizontally:

**ONE PRODUCT → ONE OBSERVATION → ONE RESOLUTION → ONE OPERATION → ONE MEASURABLE BENEFIT**

Then generalize.
